## Replacement Thesis

Cylinder Seal is not a promise of instant national transformation. It is a
prototype and policy architecture for making Iraq's economic feedback loops
visible and governable:

```text
Digital IQD evidence
  -> formal transactions and auditable public payments
  -> INDHC productive capital and private-sector crowd-in
  -> infrastructure, open-source rail, industry, green power, water, food,
     tourism, civic work, and cultural production
  -> booked cash, public benefits, resilience, skills, and social trust
  -> Treasury levy, reinvestment, ministry service contracts, and citizen
     dividends
```

All macroeconomic and policy claims should be treated as scenario-model outputs
until validated by independent legal, fiscal, economic, security, and social
review.
